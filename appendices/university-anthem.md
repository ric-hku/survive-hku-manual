# 香港大学校歌《港大礼赞》

{% embed url="https://lib.hku.hk/muslib/HKUanthem.html" %}

### 一、介绍

首演于 1912 年 3 月 11 日大学及本部大楼开幕典礼。

* 原作曲及指挥 Composer and Conductor：圣约翰座堂管风琴师典曼 · 傅勒 Denman Fuller (FRCO, LRAM, 1880 – 1925)
* 拉丁文作词 Latin Verses：前港督金文泰爵士 Sir Cecil Clementi (GCMG, KStJ, FRGS, 1875 – 1947)
* 演奏 Performance：
  * 圣约翰座堂合唱团 Choir of the St John’s Cathedral
  * 爱乐协会合唱团 Chorus of the Philharmonic Society
  * 国王直属约克郡轻步兵团第一营全编制军乐团 Full Military Band of the 1st Battalion, King’s Own Yorkshire Light Infantry

直至上世纪三十年代，大学的正式场合均沿用此校歌。但战后被淡忘，存放于大学档案处的校歌乐谱，渐渐成为看得见而听不到的历史文物。

为庆祝百周年校庆，此曲被重新谱曲并录制。

* 制作 Producer：蔡宽量教授 Prof Daniel K L Chua
* 编曲 Orchestration：陈庆恩博士 Dr Chan Hing-Yan
* 指挥 Conductor：叶咏诗 Yip Wing-sie (BBS, JP)
* 合唱团指挥 Choir Master：郑基恩 Ronnie Cheng
* 演奏 Performers：
  * 香港小交响乐团 Hong Kong Sinfonietta
  * 香港大学学生会合唱团 Hong Kong University Students' Union Choir
  * 拔萃男书院、拔萃女书院合唱团 DBS DGS Senior Mixed Choir
* 项目经理 Project Manager：Sharon Lu
* 录音工程师 Recording Engineer：Clement Pong

现时，此校歌会在各正式典礼中（如新生入学礼及颁授学位典礼）主礼队伍进场和退场时播放或演奏。通常而言，只有校歌的最后一段会被采用。

### 二、歌词

> 英译：佚名，于 1912 年

Finis hic operum! Domus\
Stat potens Academia,\
Unde ab occiduis recens\
Ampliore flust plagis\
Mox doctrina meatu.

> Here end our labours!> \
> Strong stand the buildings of the University,> \
> whence modern learning soon will flow> \
> from western land in more ample course.
>
> 差幸鸠工此日成> \
> 磐基永固耀辰星> \
> 西来新学传逾博> \
> 从此文澜赖汝荣

Fons ubi est sapientia?\
Et, Scientia, qua lates?\
Pontus has negat in suis\
Subditas latebris, negat\
Has se Terra tenere.

> Where is the fountain of wisdom?> \
> And how, O science, art thou hidden?> \
> The Sea denies that these are concealed> \
> in his hiding-place> \
> and the Earth denies that she contains them.
>
> 敢问奚方探智珠> \
> 试询格致匿何区> \
> 沧溟不解寻源处> \
> 大地偏无爱宝隅

En! Dei reverentia\
Hac scientia! Qui malis\
Abstinet, sapit. Hoc diu\
Munere assidue valentem\
Exercete iuventam!

> Lo! The fear of God - that is science!> \
> Whoso abstains from evil, he is wise.> \
> Long and earnestly may ye train> \
> youth's vigour in this duty!
>
> 格致功徙敬畏天> \
> 居心远恶智方圆> \
> 栽培幼壮行斯志> \
> 持久殷勤尚勉旃

Pandite ostia! Iam Deo\
Gratias agimus. Dei\
Semper auxilio novum\
Splendeat sapientia\
Lumen ex Oriente! AMEN

> Fling open the gates!> \
> Now we give thanks to God.> \
> By God's grace> &#x20;may the new light of wisdom> \
> ever shine out from the East!> &#x20;AMEN
>
> 入德重门此际开> \
> 天恩高厚谢滋培> \
> 新明智烛凭天眷> \
> 东耀长光永不灰> \
> 诚心所愿
